# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented here. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and the project uses [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [Unreleased]

Work toward 0.4.0: Studio Pieces & Glyph Art (#213–222), one new category built on a shared glyph engine. No breaking changes so far.

### Added

- **Studio Pieces & Glyph Art**, a new category (#213–222) with its own sidebar group, and its foundations: `src/lib/glyph/` (glyph ramps and `pickGlyph`, cached per-cell image sampling, glyph sprite atlases blitted with `drawImage` instead of per-cell `fillText`, 2×2/4×4/8×8 Bayer matrices with `orderedDither`, seven named palettes from dark to light, and a shared canvas loop with a DPR cap of 2, resize handling, offscreen and hidden-tab pausing and a still frame under reduced motion) and `src/lib/pixel-art/` (six original 16×16 desktop icons, a recolorable pixel avatar and a crisp SVG renderer).
- 26 original demo images in `public/studio/` (project stills, illustrated portraits, grayscale glyph-map sources and dithered pixel scenes), generated by `scripts/generate-studio-images.ts` (`bun run studio:images`).
- **Studio Pieces & Glyph Art**: Glyph Map Card (#220), an image redrawn as a living glyph map in a rounded card. Cells re-roll toward neighboring densities, and near the pointer they scramble, grow and push outward before settling. Below sits a colored panel (green, indigo, ember or mono) with a label, an Add card toggle, a serif title, location, watchers and an Expand button that morphs the card into a larger dialog with a caption. Classic, binary or shade-block ramps; a built-in sphere study when no image is given.
- **Studio Pieces & Glyph Art**: ASCII Shark Sea (#219), a side-view ocean in glyphs. Hammerheads (T-shaped heads), great whites (heavy bodies, fuller tails) and reef sharks (black fin tips) patrol on follow-the-leader spines with a swaying body and tail, rasterized every frame against a side profile with dorsal and pectoral fins, gill slits and an eye; they turn past the edges, often at a new depth, where they are smaller and fainter. Fish schools flock as boids, scatter from passing sharks and the cursor and regroup; a click drops sinking bait that fish and then a shark come for. Bubbles rise through soft light rays over a depth tint, in light or dark palettes or your own colors.
- **Studio Pieces & Glyph Art**: Palette Dither Field (#218), 3–5 layered sine and noise waves ordered-dithered (8×8 Bayer) to any palette of 2–8 colors, quantized to the palette's steps and dithered between the two nearest. Pointer ripples, a `direction`, `scale`, `speed` and `pixelSize`, and `ref.setPalette()` (or a new `palette` prop) that crossfades palettes with a dither of its own in about 400ms without restarting the waves. Falls back to a CSS gradient of the palette.
- **Studio Pieces & Glyph Art**: Clothesline Cards (#216), profile cards clipped to a sagging string, each a damped pendulum with a little wind at its own phase and a tug from its neighbors. The cursor pushes them aside as it passes, dragging a card swings it and pulls the string down, and hovering sweeps a holographic shimmer that follows the pointer. A click or tap flips a card in 3D to a bio, skills and links. A section header with an accent line and a team counter; horizontal scrolling with snap on narrow containers.
- **Studio Pieces & Glyph Art**: Pixel Bloom Select (#217), a select whose list grows out of colored pixels: an ordered-dither threshold sweeps top to bottom and resolves a field of pixels in the selected option's palette into crisp DOM options, and on close the options solidify into pixels that fall away. The highlighted option tints the field toward its own palette. Full listbox behavior (arrows, type-ahead, Enter, Escape, focus return); a plain fade under reduced motion.
- **Studio Pieces & Glyph Art**: Pane Stack Portfolio (#214), projects as large panes standing in a diagonal row that recedes into depth, all driven by one Motion value: wheel, drag with momentum, arrow keys and a scrubber move through the stack on a spring, and clicking a back pane brings it forward. Filter chips with counts re-stack the panes, an Index view lays every project out in a grid with hover previews, and the front pane morphs (shared layout) into a detail dialog with a player, a meta table, previous/next and a call to action. A header slot (owner pill, links, booking status with a live dot, local time and a Dark toggle); a vertical swipe deck below 560px.
- **Studio Pieces & Glyph Art**: Retro Desktop Hero (#213), a portfolio hero that is a tiny retro desktop: original pixel-art icons you can select, open and drag (snapping to a grid, positions remembered in localStorage), windows that drag by their title bar, come to the front, minimize toward the taskbar, maximize and close, a hello window with a pixel avatar, availability chip and Copy email, Book a call and See projects buttons, built-in Projects, Resume, Photos, Music, Terminal (CRT Terminal, flat) and Trash windows, and a taskbar with a profile button, window buttons, a clock and a theme toggle. Pixel Landscape is the default wallpaper; icons become a grid and windows full-screen sheets on narrow containers.
- Geist Pixel (from the `geist` package, not preloaded) as `--font-geist-pixel` on the site, for pixel labels.
- **Studio Pieces & Glyph Art**: Flow Line Chat Card (#221), an animated feature card for a community chat product: flowing blue lines (an exported `FlowLines` helper whose dashes flow with CSS while a small loop bends the curves toward the pointer) behind a mini channel where messages arrive one by one after a typing indicator, reactions pop in and count up and a system line marks someone joining. Hovering a message outlines it and shows a react, reply and more toolbar.
- **Studio Pieces & Glyph Art**: Multiplayer Canvas Card (#222), the companion card for a real-time collaboration product: named cursors follow scripted Bézier paths over a small canvas, one dragging a selection box that highlights shapes, one typing into a note and one opening a comment thread, while dashed lines flow from each cursor to an avatar stack. Hovering speeds the scene up and adds the visitor's own cursor labelled "You". Uses `FlowLines` from Flow Line Chat Card; the two pair as a feature section.
- Geist Serif (from the `geist` package, not preloaded) as `--font-geist-serif` on the site, for display titles in components that fall back to the system serif elsewhere.

### Changed

- Version 0.4.0 in progress: the navbar badge and the New badges follow it, so components from 0.3.0 no longer show New. The landing page showcases 0.3.0, the latest shipped release.

## [0.3.0] - 2026-11-07

61 components (#152–212), most of them in six new categories: 3D galleries, physical surfaces, prediction markets, usage and billing, HTML in Canvas, and navbars. No breaking changes.

### Added

- Five planned categories: 3D Galleries & Carousels, Physical Surfaces & Navigation, Prediction Markets, Usage, Billing & Agent Commerce, and HTML in Canvas, each with its own sidebar group.
- A shared gallery engine in `src/lib/gallery/`: a WebGL2 renderer for textured cards (rounded corners, soft shadows, edge fades and floor reflections in one instanced draw over a texture array with an LRU cache), `useGalleryInput` for drag with momentum and snapping, wheel, keyboard and autoplay, a hidden DOM list with a polite live region for screen readers and keyboard users, and a CSS scroll-snap fallback when WebGL is unavailable.
- 24 original placeholder images in `public/gallery/`, generated by `scripts/generate-gallery-images.ts`.
- **3D Galleries & Carousels**: Curved Wall (#153), image panels on the inside of a cylinder (flat strip to tight drum) with drift, step or no autoplay, hover braking, dial-like snapping, a floor reflection and edge fades. Sleeve Rail (#152), a fanned rail of album sleeves whose records peek on hover and slide out to spin, with the sleeve art as the label, a scrubbable ruler and an accent color picked from the artwork. Ring Reel (#156), a slowly turning wheel of cards (leaning or upright) that zooms into an arc at the top, with a flip wave revealing accent-colored backs and content centered in the ring. Darkroom Stream (#155), photos streaming out of an empty center that develop from monochrome to color (or out of blank paper as prints), with spiral paths, adjustable perspective rush, hover catching, drag steering and an open view with a caption. Formation Gallery (#154), up to 160 cards flowing between counter-rotating rings, a golden-angle spiral, a rotating globe and a drifting field with gather, swirl or direct transitions, a kept-clear center for a headline, hover holds, an open view and group tilt toward the pointer. Depth Rail (#158), a lightweight DOM + CSS 3D coverflow (no WebGL) driven by a single Motion value, with parallax drift inside each frame, reflections, momentum throws that land on a card, trackpad scrolling, a custom caption slot and a labelled, keyboard-operable carousel. Helix Gallery (#157), cards wound on a vertical helix; Page Flip Book (#159), pages that curl as they turn (a chain of CSS 3D strips) with a spine shadow; Infinite Canvas Gallery (#160), an endless pannable masonry grid with column parallax and momentum; and Scattered Prints (#161), polaroids you can pick up, throw and double-click to enlarge.
- **Physical Surfaces & Navigation**: Card Wallet (#162), a stack of fictional payment cards in metal, black guilloche, frosted glass and solid color finishes with an original network mark, pointer- or tilt-driven light, fanning, drag-to-pocket cycling and an open view with a rolling balance and recent activity. Lens Tab Bar (#163), an iOS-style tab bar whose selection is a draggable lens that redraws what passes under it inverted, with spring-opened labels, rolling badges and full tablist semantics.
- **Prediction Markets** foundations in `src/lib/markets/`: integer math (basis-point prices, cent amounts, hundredth shares, simulated price impact, P&L), formatting (probability, ¢ prices, compact volume, time left), shared types, a hydration-safe clock and generated trader avatars, plus demo-only mock markets and a live tick engine.
- **Prediction Markets**: Market Card (#164) with a probability gauge or outcome bars, Yes/No buttons with payout previews that morph into the ticket, and open, closing, resolved and loading states; Trade Ticket (#165) with Buy/Sell, Yes/No or outcome select, quick amounts, a live summary with price impact, limit orders and a simulated confirm, success and failure flow.
- **Prediction Markets**: Probability Chart (#166), odds over time with timeframe tabs that morph the lines, a toggling legend, a snapping crosshair (pointer and keyboard), annotations and a hidden data table; Outcome Ladder (#167), outcomes that re-sort by probability with layout animation, rolling prices and expandable rows; Order Book (#168), bids and asks with cumulative depth bars, flashing size changes, the spread, a mirrored No book and click-to-fill; Mobile Trade Bar (#169), a sticky bar that hides on scroll and morphs into a draggable bottom-sheet Trade Ticket; Positions Panel (#170), open, resolved and past positions with live P&L, claims with a coin burst and an empty state; Market Leaderboard (#171), a podium and ranked list by profit, volume or accuracy with the viewer's row pinned; Resolution & Rules Card (#172), a status timeline, expandable rules, a dispute countdown and a resolved stamp; and Market Activity & Discussion (#173), a live trade stream and a comment thread with position badges, likes, replies, sorting and holder filters. All demo data is simulated and labelled as such.
- Two demo pages built from the Prediction Markets components: `/demos/prediction-market` (chart, order book or outcome ladder, sticky trade ticket or mobile trade bar, rules, activity, positions and leaderboard, with a pause switch for live data) and `/demos/prediction-feed` (market cards with topic chips, search, a watchlist filter and a card-to-ticket trade overlay).
- `useHydrated` in `src/lib/markets/use-now.ts` for text derived from timestamps that are generated relative to page load.
- **Usage, Billing & Agent Commerce** foundations in `src/lib/usage/`: shared types, money formatting in integer minor units with `Intl.NumberFormat` (USD and IDR), one stable color per category (up to six), demo-only mock data (resources, a 30-day cycle, workflows, teammates, orders) and `useMockWalletAgent`, a scripted agent that parses swap, send and approve requests into reviewable cards. A `--chart-6` token backs the sixth category color.
- **Usage, Billing & Agent Commerce**: Spend Meter (#174), a monthly usage card with a rolling total against a limit, a segmented meter by resource linked to its rows, a − / + limit stepper and amber and red states with a hatched overflow; Credit Trend (#175), used, average and projected tiles with an on-pace badge, stacked daily bars against a dashed budget, a scrubbable day card (pointer and arrow keys), category toggles that re-stack the bars and top workflows with sparklines; Credit Breakdown (#176), a plan header, a category meter that links to and filters the table, rolling KPI tiles, and a searchable, sortable workflows and teammates table with a "Show all" expand and a client-side CSV export; Printed Receipt (#177), an order receipt that feeds out of a thermal printer in steps on torn paper and gets a Paid, Pending or Refunded stamp, with card or crypto payment lines; and Wallet Agent Chat (#178), an AI wallet assistant whose replies resolve tool steps, stream text and slide in swap, send or approval cards that move through review, signature, pending and confirmed or failed in place. Everything is simulated; nothing is signed without an explicit approve click.
- **HTML in Canvas** adapter in `src/lib/html-canvas/`, the only code that touches the experimental API: feature detection (155+, 150–154, legacy), drawable canvas setup, `drawElement2D`, `uploadElementTexture`, `syncElementGeometry` (which keeps clicks, focus and selection on the drawn pixels, including through perspective) and `useHtmlCanvas` (native or fallback, paint-driven uploads, a frame loop only while animating, offscreen and hidden-tab pausing, context-loss recovery). The 150–154 paths are verified in Chromium 153 with the flag. The root layout adds the origin trial meta tag when `NEXT_PUBLIC_HTML_IN_CANVAS_OT_TOKEN` is set.
- **HTML in Canvas**: Ripple Form (#179), a live sign-up form on rippling water that stays crisp where you type; Dissolve Transition (#182), content swaps that dissolve into pixels, dither or noise; CRT Terminal (#183), a real terminal on a curved, glowing CRT or e-paper; Disintegrate Delete (#184), rows that crumble into dust with an Undo that reassembles them; Magnifier Lens (#185), a loupe over live content; Cloth Banner (#187), an announcement bar on waving cloth that calms under the pointer; Peel Card (#180), a card that curls back like paper to reveal a second live face; Liquid Glass Overlay (#188), a draggable glass toolbar that refracts the content at its rim; Floating Screen (#186), a live mini-app on a tilted laptop or phone; and Living Book (#181), a 3D book of live, selectable pages. Every one works without the API through a CSS fallback, which reduced motion always uses.
- **Shaders & Backgrounds**: ASCII Koi Pond (#189), koi built from glyph-stamped grid cells (pale scales, orange and red patches, dark markings) that swim on a follow-the-leader spine, leave slope-glyph wakes and pass under drifting lily pads; they drift toward the cursor, scatter from fast moves and race for food dropped with a click. Light and dark palettes, or your own colors.
- Five more: Shelf Gallery (#190), covers that settle onto floating wall shelves, tilt toward the pointer with a glare and fly forward into an open view; Foil Card Drift (#191), payment-style cards drifting past in 3D with a holographic foil that follows each card's turn, with drag, hover slow-down and a pause button; Icon Headline (#192), a headline with app-icon tiles set inline between words that pop in and flip through icons; Pixel Mosaic (#193), a living band of colored pixels streaming in diagonal bands with a dissolving edge and pointer warmth; and Tumbling Tags (#194), labelled blocks that drop in, tumble and settle into a tilted pile.
- Pixel Landscape (#195): a mountain range drawn as a dot mosaic, shaped by ridged noise and shaded from a 2D surface normal (shadow, mid and lit bands with accent flecks), under a dotted sky and a dithered cloud; the sun follows the pointer and relights the slopes. Ember, alpine and dusk palettes, round or square dots.
- **Navbars & Headers**, a new category built on shared helpers in `src/lib/nav/` (`useMenu` for click, hover-intent, Escape and outside-click handling, `useScrollState` for position, direction and progress of the window or a scrolling panel, and a `MobileMenu` every navbar falls back to at narrow container widths): Island Nav (#196), a compact dark pill that tightens on scroll; Float Bar (#197), a frosted floating bar with dropdowns; Split Islands (#198), menus and actions on two separate islands; Duo Panel Menu (#199), dropdowns pairing a light link list with a dark featured column; Catalog Menu (#200), a multi-column mega menu with a gliding highlight and a striped spotlight card; Ticker Header (#201), an announcement marquee that pauses on hover and folds away on scroll; Center Pill Nav (#202), links in a centered pill with a sliding highlight and an accent action pill; Morphing Dropdown Nav (#203), one shared panel that springs to each menu's size and position while content slides in the direction you moved; Scroll-Aware Nav (#204), clear over the hero, frosted on scroll, tucked away going down, with a progress line; Sheet Nav (#205), a Menu button that grows a full sheet with rising oversized links, in-place groups and bottom actions; Notch Nav (#206), the logo raised in a circle cradled by a smooth notch, with a dot gliding to the current page; Gooey Nav (#207), capsules joined by liquid necks (an SVG goo filter on a hidden copy of the row, so text stays crisp) that stretch on hover; Liquid Tab Nav (#208), a bar whose bottom edge swells under the current section and flows to the next on a spring; Overlay Menu Nav (#209), a mono Menu button opening a dark, blurred overlay with link columns, badges and image story cards; Takeover Nav (#210), a smoky glass bar whose four-dot button floods the page with color while the sections rise as giant stretched words; and Gather Nav (#211), a plain white bar that melts once the page scrolls, gathering its links into a frosted pill while the brand, search and call to action float free. All use container queries, so they adapt to their own width.
- Dither Wildlife (#212): a pixel-art background in a few dithered gray inks (or your own tones): horses galloping past at different depths, a flock of gulls that wheels around and scatters from the pointer, or a grove of round and pine trees swaying in the wind with falling leaves. Shapes are shaded softly into a small buffer, then ordered-dithered and scaled up crisp.
- `/demos/html-in-canvas`: a support panel, how to enable the flag, an adapter check (a 2D-drawn and a WebGL-drawn form you can type in and click) and all ten components with Native / Fallback switches. Category 20 pages show an Experimental notice with a status chip.
- The gallery renderer keeps texture transparency (for overlays such as a record's sheen) and can turn a card over around its own axis, and the input hook can be scrubbed from another control. Space pauses any gallery with continuous motion, and gallery roots take focus on click so Escape and the arrow keys work after pointer use.
- Shared gallery captions and previous/next controls that switch to light text on dark backgrounds.

### Changed

- The navbar badge and the New badges follow 0.3.0, so components from 0.2.0 no longer show New.

### Fixed

- Compact dollar amounts in the market helpers format the same on the server and in the browser (`$412K`, not `$412.0K` from Node), which removes a hydration mismatch when market components are server-rendered.
- Trade Ticket's success state uses a darker green so its white label meets 4.5:1 contrast, and its inactive Buy/Sell tab text is darker on the grey track.

## [0.2.0] - 2026-11-01

51 components in five new categories, built on shared foundations for WebGL2 shaders and bigint-safe web3 formatting. No breaking changes.

### Added

- Five categories in the catalog: Web3: Wallet & Identity, Web3: DeFi & Trading, Marketing Sections & Logo Clouds, Shaders & Backgrounds, and Orbits, Beams & Illustrations.
- `useShaderCanvas` (`src/lib/webgl/use-shader-canvas.ts`): WebGL2 fragment shaders on a fullscreen triangle, with a pixel-ratio cap, render scale, offscreen and hidden-tab pausing, a static frame under reduced motion, context-loss recovery and cleanup.
- GLSL snippets (hash, simplex noise, fBm, Bayer dither), a hex-to-vec3 color helper, bigint-safe web3 formatting built on viem, and mock web3 data for demos.
- Twelve original placeholder brands for demos.
- **Web3: Wallet & Identity**: Connect Wallet Button, Wallet Selector Modal, Address Pill, Network Switcher, Account Dropdown, Token Balance List, Transaction Toast, Transaction History, NFT Card and Transaction Review Modal. They're presentational (data and callbacks in, no wallet calls), use bigint math only, and every demo has a state switcher.
- **Web3: DeFi & Trading**: Token Swap Widget, Token Selector Modal, Cross-chain Bridge, Slippage Settings, Token Amount Input, Candlestick Chart, Live Price Ticker, Staking Card, Liquidity Pool Card and Gas Fee Selector. The swap widget composes Token Amount Input, Token Selector Modal and Slippage Settings; amounts, rates and fees use bigint math only.
- **Marketing Sections & Logo Clouds**: Logo Grid Hover Reveal, Dual Logo Marquee, Logo Cloud Blur Cycle, Spotlight Logo Grid, Testimonial Wall, Feature Bento Section, Pricing Section, Stats Section, FAQ Section and CTA with Border Beam. Feature Bento, Pricing, Stats and FAQ reuse existing components (Sparkline, Spring Switch, Typewriter, Activity Rings, Shimmer Text, Rolling Digits, Number Ticker, Bouncy Accordion).
- **Shaders & Backgrounds**: Cloud Shader, Mesh Gradient, Grain Gradient, Dithered Waves, Liquid Ripple, Dot Matrix Field, Light Rays, GPU Aurora, Glow Orb and Pixel Reveal Image, all on `useShaderCanvas` with CSS fallbacks, offscreen pausing and a still frame under reduced motion. Plus ASCII Aquarium Background: a 2D-canvas glyph aquarium on grid paper whose hero creature drifts toward the cursor and orbits a chat input while listening or thinking.
- **Orbits, Beams & Illustrations**: Orbiting Circles, Integration Hub, Animated Beam, Radar Sweep, Dotted Globe, Ripple Rings, Icon Cloud, Border Beam, Node Network and Animated Grid Pattern. CSS-keyframe pieces ship their keyframes through React 19 hoisted styles; canvas and 3D pieces pause offscreen and hold still under reduced motion.
- `useShaderCanvas` accepts sampler2D textures (images, canvases, video), uniforms as a per-frame function, and returns the shader clock as `time`. On software WebGL (SwiftShader, llvmpipe) it skips compiling and shows the CSS fallback, unless `allowSoftware` is set.
- Shared web3 helpers for registry items: generated token, chain, wallet and address avatars, `RollingValue` for rolling pre-formatted amounts with green/red flashes, and shared `Token`, `Chain`, `WalletOption` and `TxStatus` types.
- A version badge in the navbar and a `/changelog` page.
- Landing page: a "What's new in v0.2.0" section with live showcases (Token Swap Widget, Orbiting Circles, ASCII Aquarium Background), Logo Grid Hover Reveal under the hero, and an optional Cloud Shader hero background.

### Changed

- The "New" badge marks every component added in the latest release instead of a hand-picked list.
- Registry items can ship the new shared lib files next to a component.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-01

### Added

- 100 animated, accessible components in 10 categories: Buttons & Actions, Inputs & Forms, Toggles & Selection, Overlays & Feedback, Navigation, Text Animations, Numbers & Data Display, Cards & Layout, Media, Scroll & Backgrounds, and Loaders, AI & Widgets.
- A shadcn-compatible registry (`/r/<slug>.json`) that installs dependencies, helpers, design tokens and sibling components.
- A docs site: landing page with live featured components, a searchable gallery with a ⌘K palette, a page per component (live preview, variants, code, install commands, props table, accessibility notes) and guides for installation, theming and motion.
- Shared design tokens (colors, radius, shadows) and motion tokens (durations, easings, springs) in `src/lib/motion.ts`.
- SEO metadata, a sitemap, `robots.txt` and generated Open Graph images for the site and every component.
- An experimental WebGL2 playground at `/lab`.
